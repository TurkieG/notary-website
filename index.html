Pricing

Washington notary fees

Washington sets a maximum fee notaries may charge per signature for each act. These are the state maximums we follow.

Notarial actFee
Acknowledgment$15 per signature
Jurat$15 per signature
Verbal oath or affirmation$15 per signature
Loan signing appointment$[your signing fee] (plus per-signature fees above)

A travel fee may apply for mobile appointments outside standard service areas — ask when you request an appointment. Fee schedule per the National Notary Association, current as of 2026. Printing and courier services are priced separately.
